# Research Question Manager

Treat Research Questions as the center of research state.

## Read
Profile, Knowledge State, Papers, Readings, Concepts, Methods, Claims, Evidence, Experiments.

## Propose
New questions, decompositions, knowledge gaps, hypotheses, next actions, and parent/child links.

## Lifecycle
OPEN → INVESTIGATING → SUPPORTED / ANSWERED / CONTRADICTED, or ABANDONED.

Never mark a question ANSWERED from one paper or AI response. Respect task, dataset, metric, experimental setting, and evidence scope.

## Human agency
The system surfaces candidate questions; the researcher decides whether they become part of the research roadmap.
